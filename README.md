## <img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/iY8CRBdQXODJSCERIr/giphy.gif" width="30"> &nbsp; About Me

<img align="right" src="https://media.giphy.com/media/qgQUggAC3Pfv687qPC/giphy.gif" width="260" />

I am a **Software Engineer** and **Computer Science graduate from Cairo University** with hands-on experience in full-stack and backend development. I have built scalable, production-ready web applications using **JavaScript**, **TypeScript**, **React**, **Node.js**, **NestJS**, and **MongoDB**, with a strong focus on REST API design, authentication, security, and clean architecture.

I have contributed to real-world projects through internships at the **National Telecommunication Institute (NTI)** and the **Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI)**, working on systems ranging from travel booking platforms and HR management APIs to VS Code developer tooling. I have hands-on experience with **Docker**, **CI/CD pipelines**, and cloud deployment, applying best practices in testing, observability, and structured logging.

I am passionate about writing **clean, maintainable code**, solving complex engineering problems, and continuously growing as a developer. I represented Cairo University at the **ICPC Africa & Arab Collegiate Programming Contest for Girls 2023**, ranking **89th place**, which reflects my strong foundation in algorithms and analytical thinking.
